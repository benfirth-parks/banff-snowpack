## Reading these numbers
- Snow depth is tracked well where the plot has its own temperature and a nearby gauge; Bow Summit is
  under-predicted every season (peaks 0.3-0.7 m low): its gauge catches ~0.9x ERA5 while Sunshine catches ~1.6x,
  pointing at gauge undercatch at Bow (first candidate for a physically based correction, to be tested
  leave-one-season-out).
- Goat's Eye 2023-24 and 2024-25 have low r partly because of the evaluation sensor itself (gaps, spikes).
- Weak-layer recall is high but precision ~0.55-0.63: the model predicts weak layers that observers did not
  record. Model profiles still carry ~3x more layers than observed profiles after merging.
- Pit snow depth differs from the station sensor by 15-25 cm on average (spatial variability within the plot).
